Company Overview

AHA Resources is an Indonesia-centric diversified group facilitating tourism, trade and investment between Indonesia and international markets. Our businesses are interlinked: a tourist uses our Travel service, stays in our Hospitality network, dines at our Restaurants, shops at our Retail, and the products they love come from our Agriculture, Fisheries and Trade arms.

VISION & MISSION

Trusted, diversified, internationally connected. Connect, Develop, Facilitate, Grow.

WHY INDONESIA

17,000 islands, 270M+ people, 5 super-priority destinations, PT PMA openness.

How Partnerships Proceed

A disciplined, transparent path

Every proposal moves through the same four gates. No favours, no fog. The same discipline we apply to our own divisions.

Express Interest
Gate 01

Share your intent, capacity and horizon. We respond within five business days.
