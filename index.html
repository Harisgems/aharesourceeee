WHO WE ARE

A Diversified Platform Built on Integration, Not Isolation.

AHA Resources is a diversified business platform operating across 11 verticals: Tourism, Hospitality, Restaurants, Retail, Automotive, Agriculture, Fisheries, Import-Export, Healthcare, Legal & Corporate Consultancy, and Training & Skills Development.

We are not 11 separate companies. We are one integrated ecosystem where each business feeds the next. Our model: Resources → Expertise → Investment → Opportunities → Markets → Value.

Headquartered with Indonesia as hub and International as corridor, we connect Indonesian resources, culture, and products to global demand — and bring international expertise, capital, and tourists into Indonesia.

Company Overview

AHA Resources is an Indonesia-centric diversified group operating across 11 verticals. We facilitate tourism, trade, and investment between Indonesia and international markets. Our businesses are designed to be interlinked — a tourist uses our Travel service, stays in our Hospitality network, eats at our Restaurants, shops at our Retail, and the products they buy come from our Agriculture, Fisheries and Import-Export arms. The legal and training arms support investors who want to replicate this model.

INVESTMENT APPROACH

Opportunity → Feasibility → Structure → Partnership → Investment → Growth.

Not just capital — facilitation. We bring the opportunity, check feasibility, structure the business, find partners, invest together, and grow.

FACILITATION MODEL

Idea → Market Research → Legal Structure (PT PMA) → Location → Licenses → Build-out → Staffing (Training) → Supply Chain (Agriculture/Fisheries) → Operations (Hospitality/Restaurants) → Marketing (Travel) → Export (Trade/Retail).

01
OPPORTUNITY IDENTIFICATION
Identify gaps in Indonesia tourism corridor — no premium homestay, no seafood restaurant, no organic supply.
02
FEASIBILITY & MARKET STUDY
Tourist footfall, competitor analysis, cost structure, regulatory check, ROI projection from operating businesses.
03
STRUCTURE & LEGAL
PT PMA or JV structure, licenses, KITAS, property lease, compliance roadmap. Handled by Legal vertical.
04
PARTNERSHIP & NETWORK
Connect with local partners, government, suppliers, buyers, tourism boards. Network is our moat.
05
INVESTMENT & SETUP
Capital deployment, build-out, staffing via Training academy, supply chain setup, SOPs.
06
GROWTH & VALUE CREATION
Operations, marketing via Travel network, retail export, scaling to other islands, eventual exit or expansion. Integrated returns.
